# Research

These papers inform design hypotheses. They do not show that Mentis improves learning.

| Paper | Link | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Bastani et al. (2025), Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning. PNAS. | [PMC12232635](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12232635/) | In a high school math study, unrestricted GPT-4 raised practice scores but lowered later unaided scores. A tutor with learning guardrails largely prevented the drop. |
| Roediger & Karpicke (2006), Test-enhanced learning. Psychological Science. | [doi](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x) | Testing gave better recall than restudying after 2 days and 1 week. Restudying was better after 5 minutes. |
| Kapur (2014), Productive failure in learning math. Cognitive Science. | [doi](https://doi.org/10.1111/cogs.12107) | Students who attempted problems before instruction showed the same procedural skill but better conceptual understanding and transfer. |

Checked against abstracts only. The full texts were not accessible.
